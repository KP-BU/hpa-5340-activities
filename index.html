HPA 5340 Healthcare Operations and Management

Interactive activity previews for faculty review

How to review: open any activity, try it as a student would, and note any content changes you'd like. In Canvas each one will run inside the module page and report completion to the gradebook; these previews run on their own and don't record anything.
Module 1

Required or Chosen?

Drag-and-drop sort

Sorting 14 real agencies and requirements into compulsory vs. voluntary checks that students can tell licensure from accreditation before deemed status comes back in Module 7.

Outcome: Distinguish compulsory licensure, certification, and regulation from voluntary accreditation, and explain deemed status (Griffith & White Ch. 1).

Open activity
Module 2

Run the Numbers

Dropdown worked case

A unit manager's numbers walked through step by step makes the productivity formulas concrete before the Assessing Employee Productivity simulation.

Outcome: Analyze and evaluate the operating process using productivity measures (single vs. multiple factor, labor hours per unit of service, benchmarking) (Ch. 3 & 5; Productivity & Performance Management deck).

Open activity
Module 3

Inside the Board Meeting

Drag-the-words cloze

A board-meeting narrative where students place the right role or process term (NIFO, privileging, peer review) targets the 'micromanage or rubber stamp' confusion.

Outcome: Recognize board vs. management duties and the medical staff functions, including credentialing and privileging (Ch. 4, 6–8).

Open activity
Module 4

The Wrong Vial

Branching scenario

Uses the deck's own wrong-vial case: the student, as manager, classifies each nurse's behavior (human error, at-risk, reckless) and chooses console, coach, or sanction, then routes the event to reporting.

Outcome: Apply a validated decision-making framework (the Just Culture decision tree) to errors and near misses, and connect it to incident reporting and RCA.

Open activity
Module 5

Supply Chain Strategy Planner

Guided documentation tool

Mirrors the Strategic Planning class exercise as a structured planner (supply chain problem → stakeholders → SWOT → three SMART goals → scorecard measures) with a downloadable summary.

Outcome: Develop and evaluate plans for changing the operating process within the organization and its supply chain (stakeholder analysis, SWOT, SMART goals, balanced scorecard).

Open activity
Module 6

Spot the Legal Risk

Mark the words

Students read a nurse-manager interview transcript and mark every question or remark that creates EEOC, FMLA, or at-will risk.

Outcome: Understand the principles of healthcare HR: lawful recruitment and selection, employment law, and labor relations (Ch. 11).

Open activity
Module 7

Surveyor at the Door

Dialog cards

Each card is a surveyor finding from a tracer (blocked egress, fire door failure, incomplete generator logs, medical gas storage, negative pressure failure, NPSG gaps); the back gives the standard and the leader's fix.

Outcome: Ensure compliance with accrediting and regulatory requirements for the environment of care and life safety (tracer methodology, NPSGs, NFPA 101; Ch. 12).
